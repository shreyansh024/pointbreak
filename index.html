A content day, built on your wake time

WhenWhat

Sunday batch shoot · 2 hours

  1. Pick 5 ideas from the Ideas tab and schedule them Mon–Sat. Mix: 2 education, 1 Weigh-in, 1 shop or Sanisha, 1 funny.
  2. Write the hook and 3 beats for each on paper. Say the hook out loud; if it doesn't sound like you at the counter, change it.
  3. Set up once: plum velvet for silver, ivory cloth for gold, the 0.01 g scale, loupe, phone on tripod near the window.
  4. Shoot all the close-ups first (pieces, stamps, scale), then all the face-to-camera parts in one outfit.
  5. Rough-cut in CapCut the same evening. Save each as a draft named with its post date.
  6. Shoot 3 spare B-roll clips (shop, hands, scale) for days when nothing gets filmed.

Shoot better reels

Light

  • Window light from the side, at 45°. Never under the shop's overhead tube lights.
  • For sparkle, move a small LED or the phone torch slowly across the piece while you film.
  • White card on the shadow side to lift it; black card to kill reflections on polished gold.

Phone settings

  • 4K at 30 fps; 60 fps for slow-motion sparkle.
  • Tap and hold to lock focus and exposure before you start.
  • Use 2× for close-ups, not 0.5× (wide lenses distort jewellery). Wipe the lens every time.

Framing

  • Vertical 9:16. Keep faces and text in the middle 60% so captions and buttons don't cover them.
  • Tripod or an overhead rig for flat-lays; handheld only for walk-throughs.
  • Clean hands and nails in every close-up; the piece is the hero.

Sound

  • Clip-on mic for talking parts, or shoot before the shop opens.
  • Trending audio only for funny reels; education reels use your voice.
  • Burn in captions: most people watch muted.

Your signature

  • The 0.01 g scale in every Weigh-in and explainer.
  • Spec card on screen for weight, length and purity, in a mono font.
  • End card for one second: the wordmark and "Stay jainuine."

Edit

  • Cut every pause. A new shot every 2–3 seconds.
  • No more than 6 words per caption line, 2 lines at a time.
  • One cover style per series so the grid reads like a shelf.

Before you hit post

  • Hook said in the first 2 seconds, with text on screen.
  • Under 40 seconds. Captions burned in. No price, making charge or margin anywhere, including the caption.
  • Collab invite sent to the right account (gold and weddings → Pooja, silver and drops → Sanisha).
  • Caption: hook again, one useful line, one question for the comments, 3–5 hashtags.
  • Post at your slot (about 1 PM or 7–9 PM). Reply to every comment in the first hour.
  • Next morning, note the saves. After two weeks, do more of whichever series gets the most.

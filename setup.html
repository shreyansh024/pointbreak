Pointbreak on your iPhone

1 · Install the app

  1. Open this link in Safari: shreyansh024.github.io/pointbreak
  2. Tap Share (the square with an arrow) → Add to Home Screen → Add.
  3. Open Pointbreak from the new icon. The gold and silver rates at the top work straight away.
  4. To load your planner, paste your connection link once. To get it, open the Pointbreak Google Sheet in the Google Sheets app, go to the Settings tab and copy cell B3.

2 · One-tap "I'm awake"

  1. Open Shortcuts → + → Add Action → Get Contents of URL.
  2. URL: your connection link with &action=wake added on the end.
  3. Add the action Show Result. Name the shortcut I'm awake.
  4. Pick how to trigger it:
    • a Shortcuts widget on your home screen,
    • Settings → Accessibility → Touch → Back Tap → Double Tap (two taps on the back of the phone),
    • "Hey Siri, I'm awake",
    • or Automation → Alarm → Is Stopped, which logs it automatically when you stop your alarm.
  5. Make a second shortcut with &action=sleep for Going to bed.

3 · Reminders that ring when the app is closed

  1. Settings → Calendar → Accounts → Add Account → Other → Add Subscribed Calendar.
  2. Server: your connection link with &action=ics added on the end → Next → Save.
  3. Open that account and switch Remove Alerts off.

Each task alerts 10 minutes before its time. Tasks without a time alert at 9:00, and daily routines alert at their set time.
